Personal tool. Signals are automated technical setups on 5-minute candles from free Yahoo Finance data (can lag 1–3 minutes). Entry, stop-loss and target are index (spot) levels; the suggested option is the at-the-money strike for the nearest expiry. An at-the-money option moves roughly half as many points as the index, and loses value with time. Past results don't guarantee future ones. Not investment advice.
